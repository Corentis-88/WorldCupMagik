How to Use

Simple guide for generating a betslip.
Open App
Quick Start Generate a betslip
  1. Enter your total stake.
  2. Choose Date From.
  3. Choose Date To.
  4. Move the risk slider.
  5. Tap Refresh Central Scan.
  6. Read the Top Picks betslip.
Top Picks Uses Risk

This is the main betslip. It uses your date range and risk slider to choose the Single, Double, Trixie, and accumulators.

Pick of the Day Ignores Risk

This section chooses the most likely Trixie, 4-leg accumulator, and 8-leg accumulator from the same date range.

Risk Slider What it means

Lower risk looks for steadier, higher-confidence bets. Higher risk allows bigger prices and more adventurous value picks. It still uses the database and does not deliberately pick nonsense.

Database Updates daily

The central database updates 7 times per day. If the screen says Data Gathering: Come back in 5, wait a few minutes and refresh.
