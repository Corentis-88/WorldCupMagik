How to Use

Simple guide for generating a betslip.
Open App
Quick Start Generate a betslip
  1. Open the app and wait for the latest database to load.
  2. Enter the stake you want on each recommended bet.
  3. Choose Date From.
  4. Choose Date To.
  5. Move the risk slider.
  6. Read the Top Picks betslip.
Top Picks Uses Risk

This is the main betslip. It uses your date range and risk slider to choose the Single, Double, Trixie, and accumulators. The percentage is an AI rating, not a guaranteed win chance.

Picks of the Day Ignores Risk

This section chooses the highest-rated Single, Double, Trixie, 4-leg accumulator, and 8-leg accumulator the same date range can support. It ignores the risk slider.

Risk Slider What it means

Lower risk looks for steadier, higher-confidence bets. Higher risk allows bigger prices and more adventurous value picks. It still uses the database and does not deliberately pick nonsense.

Database Updates 7 times per day

The database updates 7 times per day automatically. There is no manual scan button. If the screen says Data Gathering: Come back in 5, wait a few minutes and reopen the page.
